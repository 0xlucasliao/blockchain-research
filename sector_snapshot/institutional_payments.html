Institutional payments

April 2026 · B2B stablecoin payments hit $226B in 2025 — 733% YoY growth (McKinsey/Artemis). These are the flows live in production, and the gaps that still require off-chain infrastructure.

High demand · not yet practical

Settle a securities trade and its cash payment at exactly the same moment, on-chain
Atomic DvP · public-chain security token + CBDC/stablecoin cash · replaces T+1 CSD settlement

Why blocked: CSDs and RTGS systems are the legal record of title and settlement finality. On-chain transfer is not legal title transfer without rule changes or law reform. Wholesale CBDC pilots exist, but not broad production equivalence to central bank settlement money.

Banks settle with each other in real time across currencies — without pre-funding accounts in every country
Nostro-free multicurrency netting · replaces pre-funded correspondent balances

Why blocked: True nostro elimination needs interoperable settlement-grade central bank assets across jurisdictions. No universal wholesale CBDC exists; consortium models still rely on prefunding and limited participant sets.

ERP automatically pays supplier on delivery confirmation from sensor/certificate
Programmable B2B payment · oracle trigger · automated AP settlement

Why blocked: Oracle liability remains unresolved, ERP systems are bank-API/ISO-native, and on-chain irreversibility conflicts with standard corporate AP recovery expectations.

Issue a corporate bond on public chain with atomic coupons and open secondary trading
On-chain issuance · ERC-1400/Polymesh style rails · replaces legacy CSD issuance stack

Why blocked: Legal ownership still ties to CSD registers in most jurisdictions, while transfer-level compliance needs permissioned identity enforcement that public permissionless rails do not natively provide.
