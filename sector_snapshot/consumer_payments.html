Sector Snapshot

Consumer payments

April 2026 · What already works end-to-end, and a few high-leverage flows still missing.

Flows possible today

P2P stablecoin send
Sender wallet → public chain → recipient wallet
Crypto-funded card spend at a card merchant
User wallet → issuer / swap → Visa or Mastercard → acquirer → merchant (fiat settlement)
Checkout with stablecoin via PSP
Shopper wallet → on-chain transfer → PSP / acquirer → merchant dashboard (often still fiat out)
QR wallet-to-merchant (fiat-backed)
Customer app → wallet operator → acquirer → merchant bank

High demand · not yet practical

Tap-to-pay stablecoin at any POS terminal
Phone NFC → chain → merchant account (no card scheme)

Why blocked: POS kernels, schemes, and licensing are card-centric; merchants rarely accept raw chain addresses at the terminal.

Self-custody subscription pull (set-and-forget)
Merchant → recurring debit → user wallet allowance

Why blocked: Chains are pull-unfriendly; allowance UX and revocation risk; banks do not treat on-chain pulls as ACH-equivalent.

Universal chargeback / dispute layer on-chain
Buyer → payment → reversible finality → merchant

Why blocked: Finality models differ from card networks; no shared dispute arbiter with merchant adoption.
