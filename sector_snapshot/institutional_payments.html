Institutional payments

April 2026 · B2B and bank rails that already clear value, versus atomic institutional settlement still out of reach.

Flows possible today

Cross-border supplier invoice (SWIFT)
Corporate AP → originating bank → correspondent network → beneficiary bank → supplier account
High-value domestic wire (RTGS)
Payer bank → central bank RTGS → payee bank (same currency, business-day windows)
Tokenised money-market fund as collateral (within existing custody)
Investor → transfer agent / custodian → triparty repo rail → bank balance sheet
Trade finance with bank-issued digital instrument + SWIFT data
Exporter → advising bank → messages (MT/MX) → importer bank → settlement leg (cash or credit)

High demand · not yet practical

Atomic DvP: public-chain leg with same-day CBDC / reserve finality
Security token + cash token → simultaneous settlement → RTGS recognition

Why blocked: Legal finality and CSD interfaces remain RTGS-centric; public-chain legs rarely treated as settlement-grade without new law + bridges.

Real-time nostro-free multicurrency netting across Tier-1 peers
Bank A ↔ Bank B ↔ Bank C → single shared ledger position → EoD not required

Why blocked: Bilateral credit and prefunding rules; CBDC / tokenised reserves not interoperable across all jurisdictions yet.

Programmable B2B payment from ERP with on-chain condition (delivery oracle)
SAP / Oracle → payment intent → oracle attestation → settlement

Why blocked: ERP payment files expect bank APIs; oracle liability and reversibility expectations do not map 1:1 to chain finality.
